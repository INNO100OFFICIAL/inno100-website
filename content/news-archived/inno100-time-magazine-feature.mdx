## Global Media Spotlight

On **August 5**, a feature on INNO100 was published on **TIME.com**. The article presented INNO100 as a representative example of how global innovation can move into real-life, in-person settings, introducing INNO100's brand story and ongoing exploration to readers worldwide.

This international perspective offers a closer look at what INNO100 has been building.

*Source: TIME.com Branded Content, August 5, 2026.*

## A New Paradigm for Innovation Retail

### Why Has INNO100 Become an Innovation Case Study of International Interest?

We are living through a period of **rapidly accelerating innovation**. From AI and robotics to smart wearables and digital fabrication, new technologies and products continue to push boundaries. Innovation has long been a major focus for industry professionals, entrepreneurs and investors.

Today, that interest is moving beyond professional circles. More consumers and urban explorers are actively seeking out new products and experiences, eager to encounter the future as it takes shape.

Innovative products are moving from industry exhibitions into consumer markets, and from specialist conversations into everyday urban life. People want more than an explanation of a technological breakthrough — **they want to experience** the products reshaping how we work, live and create.

The TIME.com feature highlighted INNO100's role within the global innovation ecosystem: **bringing products from China and around the world into real consumer settings**, where brands and users can engage directly.

### From Development to Market

From our perspective, bringing an innovative product from development to market requires **real users, relevant consumer settings and strong industry participation**.

INNO100 serves as an important part of this journey: helping global innovation brands gain closer access to the Chinese market, enabling users to experience products earlier, and feeding real-world insights back into continuous product development.

## A Global First: "Innovation Retail 4.0"

### Redefining the Innovation Retail Experience

INNO100 is reimagining conventional technology retail through its globally pioneering **"Innovation Retail 4.0" model**. Built around global innovation products, personalized customization services and immersive event experiences, the model invites users to move beyond purchasing and become active participants in experience and creation.

INNO100 is also **the world's only innovation retail space** to bring this diversified business structure together within a premier commercial complex.

![INNO100 Innovation Retail Space](/images/news/picture1.png "INNO100 Global Innovation Flagship Store showcasing the Innovation Retail 4.0 experience.")

The store goes beyond the role of a conventional sales outlet — it serves as an entry point for global innovation products into everyday life. Visitors can:

- **Try products** in real-world settings before purchase
- **Share feedback** directly with brands and developers
- **Participate in the creative process** through customization
- **Complete personalized customization on site**, transforming standardized goods into distinctive expressions of their own ideas

Brands, in turn, gain access to real users, innovation communities and potential industry partners.

![Visitors Exploring Products at INNO100](/images/news/picture2.png "Interactive product experience zones where customers can try and explore the latest innovations hands-on.")

INNO100 has developed a diversified business portfolio spanning:

INNO100's impact extends beyond the store itself. As products, users and creators continue to meet here, its reach increasingly extends into the wider city.

The aim is to bring **global frontier innovation into everyday life**. Innovation can take place not only in laboratories, industrial parks and professional exhibitions, but also in shopping centers, neighborhoods and cultural spaces.

INNO100 makes innovation a more participatory part of urban life and offers the public a tangible way to experience Shenzhen's innovation landscape.
