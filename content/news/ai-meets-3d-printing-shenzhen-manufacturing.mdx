## AI Meets 3D Printing: INNO100 Featured in Shenzhen Manufacturing Report

A recent Sohu report on the quiet transformation underway in Shenzhen's manufacturing sector opens its story at INNO100. It describes a group of schoolchildren at the Shenzhen Bay flagship store watching a metal-cutting machine trace a titanium blank while a nearby display streamed the underlying 3D model — and, once the AI-optimized structure finished machining, a software prompt reporting the result: "重量减轻 18.7%，刚度提升 23%" ("18.7% lighter, 23% stiffer"). The report uses the moment to introduce a broader look at how AI is reshaping 3D printing and design across the city.

### A Public Window into an Industry Shift

The report's central argument is that AI is moving from a peripheral add-on to the operating layer of the entire 3D printing production line — informing structural design, materials research, and manufacturing scheduling. It cites examples spanning medical devices, construction, and consumer goods to make the case that Shenzhen is leading this shift. INNO100 is framed as one of the places where the public can see that shift in person, rather than read about it secondhand.

### About the Report

The report cites figures and claims from industry sources and companies it covers regarding production speed, material testing, and market share; these are the report's figures, not claims INNO100 makes about its own operations. Readers interested in the full data and sourcing can read the original report on Sohu.
