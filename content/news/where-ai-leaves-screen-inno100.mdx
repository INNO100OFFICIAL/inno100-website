## Where AI Leaves the Screen: INNO100 Brings Global Tech Innovation to Real Life

INNO100 is a permanent interactive destination in Shenzhen Bay Culture Square designed to make artificial intelligence tangible and accessible to the public. As described, "the public can see the digital hype, but they cannot feel how it actually reshapes their daily reality"—a gap INNO100 aims to close.

![INNO100 Space Design](/images/news/time-inno100-space.jpg "The 188,000-square-meter immersive showroom with interactive zones")

### Space Design

The 188,000-square-meter venue operates as an immersive showroom with two zones. Zone A focuses on customization workstations and smart-home environments, while Zone B serves as a community hub with tech-art installations and event spaces.

### Featured Products

Notable installations include the Eight Sleep Pod 5 (valued at $1.5 billion), Unitree Robotics consumer-grade robots, and Bambu Lab 3D printers—all demonstrating how AI integrates into everyday life.

![Featured Products at INNO100](/images/news/time-inno100-products.jpg "Global innovation products on display")

### Strategic Function

Beyond retail, INNO100 operates as a product launch venue through its "First Launch" template, hosting in-person debuts for hardware companies targeting early adopters. The space has partnered with over 20 brands.

![Community Hub at INNO100](/images/news/time-inno100-community.jpg "Tech-art installations and event spaces")

### Founders

Baron Wang and Wayne Zhang of Longham Group, along with Henri Pang (Kickstarter China Strategic Representative), developed INNO100 based on the principle that consumer tech success depends on whether people can "touch it, understand it, and embrace it into their daily life."
